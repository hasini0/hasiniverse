// why i built it

the worry

"did she take her pills?" is a question families ask every single evening.

About 1 in 4 adults over 65 lives alone, and missed medicine is one of the most common reasons they end up in hospital. Their kids either call every day or find out too late.

But there's already a smart speaker sitting in the kitchen, and grandparents are happy to talk to it. It just needs a memory, good timing, and the judgment to know when to call for help.

so i gave it all three ♡

// a day with margaret, 78

what it actually does

Margaret lives alone. Her daughter Priya and son Dev live far away. She just talks to Alexa like she always has.

  1. 8:00 pm
    check_nudges()

    "Margaret, it's time for your Metformin."

    Nobody asked. Alexa speaks first, and only once per dose.

  2. 8:02 pm
    log_medication("Metformin")

    "I just took my Metformin."

    Logged. The bottle is down to 5 days, so Priya gets asked to pick up a refill.

  3. 8:15 pm
    daily_checkin()

    "I'm a bit dizzy and only slept 4 hours."

    Dizziness is a red flag. Priya and Dev both get a high-urgency alert, and Priya's phone turns red.

  4. 8:21 pm
    get_family_messages()

    Priya: "Call you at 7? ❤️"

    Alexa reads it out loud, and Priya sees "heard ✓".

  5. next week
    prepare_doctor_visit()

    "Get me ready for Dr. Chen."

    One page with missed doses, mood, sleep and her symptoms in her own words.

// the judgment part

when does family hear?

After every chat, CareCircle checks these rules on its own. No black box, just rules you can read.

Fall, dizziness, chest pain, confusion or short of breathhigh · everyone
2 or more missed doses in one daymedium · everyone
Low mood two check-ins in a rowmedium · everyone
Under 5 hours of sleep two nights runninglow · one person
5 days or less of a medicine leftlow · one person

The same alert never repeats within 6 hours, so nobody starts ignoring them.

// under the hood

how it's built

MCP server in Python

15 tools any AI assistant can call, using the Model Context Protocol. Every tool returns what Alexa says, what a screen shows, and the raw data.

Memory in SQLite

Everything is saved, so "how's Mom been this week?" has a real answer, even after a restart.

Alexa+ brain on Bedrock

The agent runs on Amazon Bedrock. There's also a browser simulator of the kitchen, Priya's phone and every request live.
